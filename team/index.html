How I built a website company run by 6 bots

One job per bot. One human. That's me, and I press Send.

2 minute read, promise ↓

I build websites for local businesses that don't have one. You know the type: great reviews, loyal regulars, and when you search for them online there's nothing to click.

I don't have a team of people doing this. I have 6 bots (Grok Bot agents), and each one has exactly one job. I'm the only human in the loop. The bots do the work, and I read what's about to go out and decide if it does.

Why it works

01

One job per bot.

A bot that does one thing is easier to check, easier to fix, and easier to trust. When something's off, I know exactly which one to look at.

02

Nothing ships without a check.

There's a gate between the work and the outside world. Fast doesn't matter if the site looks cheap, so the Checker and the CEO both have to say yes first.

03

A human sends every message.

The bots draft, I send. Every email has my name on it, so I read it before it goes. That's the part I won't hand off.

04

Start with one bot, then add.

You don't need six on day one. Get one job working well, then give the next job to a new bot.

How you could start

  1. Pick one task you repeat every week.
  2. Give one bot that single job, and nothing else.
  3. Check its work yourself. Once you trust it, add the next bot.
